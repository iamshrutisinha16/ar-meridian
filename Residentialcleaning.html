Homeowner FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our residential cleaning packages and safety measures.

Do I need to stay home during the cleaning process?

Not at all! Many of our residential clients safely hand over duplicate keys or leave instructions while they are out at work, running errands, or travelling.

Are your cleaning chemicals safe for children and pets?

Yes, absolutely. We strictly utilize eco-friendly, non-toxic, skin-safe, and biodegradable cleaning products that pose zero health risks to your family members or pets.

What equipment do your home cleaning teams bring?

Our professional team arrives fully equipped with single-disc floor scrubbing machines, high-power vacuum cleaners, microfiber cloths, and specialized cleaning liquids.

How long does a full home cleaning service take?

Depending on the size and layout of your residence (ranging from 1 BHK to 3+ BHK villas), it usually takes anywhere between 4 to 8 hours with our expert crew.
